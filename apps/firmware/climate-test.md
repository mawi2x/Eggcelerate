session limit have been removed. Use the existing preparation-to-incubation flow.

See [the current preparation guide](../../docs/guide/incubator-preparation-guide.md)
and [main firmware instructions](Eggcelerate/README.md) for upload, automatic
regulation, readiness notification, owner confirmation, Stop and power recovery.
