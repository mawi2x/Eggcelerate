Observe the equipment.

The page shows the GPIO command; fan rotation and relay contacts are not measured. A click or indicator LED alone does not prove the contacts conduct. With load supplies disconnected, check COM/NO/NC continuity if a meter is available.

All five outputs have independent controls and can run together. Relays switch off automatically after ten seconds. Fans run until switched off or the session ends; software timing under network stalls still needs qualification. Sessions end after two minutes or ten seconds without browser heartbeat.

This separate sketch pauses sensor/cloud/display services. Upper fan GPIO18 and lower fan GPIO19 use HIGH-on/LOW-off. IN4 is unused. GPIO4/21/23 and unassigned GPIO26 are not driven. Keep flash erasure disabled.
