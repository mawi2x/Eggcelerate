# Screen notes

Place plain explanatory notes and preview disclaimers
after the primary content, at the bottom of their screen
or active tab. This is the approved placement convention for future frontend work.

Keep the existing typography, spacing, color tokens, accessible descriptions, and
status announcements when moving a notice. Bottom means after the content in the
normal document flow; do not pin notes over the navigation or content.

Warning and information banners retain their original contextual positions,
including the Trends telemetry warning above the view tabs. Loading and fetch
error/retry messages remain near the content they describe. Inline field validation
stays associated with its field. Operational alerts retain their actionable context.

Current bottom-note examples: the Trends chart guide, the Monitor
telemetry preview note, and the Hardware preferences availability note.
